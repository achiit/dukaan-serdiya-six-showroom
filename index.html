Mujhe apne business ki website banana hai. Maine jo images attach ki hain unhe dekho (dukaan ki photos, menu, products, signboard) aur neeche diye format mein details bharo. Jo cheez images mein nahi dikh rahi uske liye reasonable guess lagao. Yeh exact format fill karo: BUSINESS_NAME: (signboard se naam ya best guess) TAGLINE: (5-10 words jo is business ki khaasiyat batayein) NICHE: (type — plumber/sweet shop/tailor/salon/restaurant/etc) LOCATION: (sheher aur neighbourhood agar dikh raha ho) YEARS_IN_BUSINESS: (appearance se andaza lagao) OWNER_NAME: (agar kahin dikh raha ho) PHONE: (agar dikh raha ho, warna FILL IN likho) WHATSAPP_NUMBER: (generally phone jaisa hi hota hai) EMAIL: (agar dikh raha ho, warna blank chhod do) FULL_ADDRESS: (agar signboard ya surroundings mein dikh raha ho) HOURS: (agar nahi dikh raha toh business type se andaza lagao) SERVICES_OR_PRODUCTS: - item 1 (price agar dikh raha ho) - item 2 (price agar dikh raha ho) DISTINCTIVE_FACT: (kuch unique — teen peedhi, area mein akela, famous dish) COLOR_VIBE: (dukaan ka feel — warm/festive/clean/earthy) INSTAGRAM_HANDLE: (agar dikh raha ho) HERO_IMAGE_URL: (blank chhod do) OTHER_IMAGE_URLS: (blank chhod do) OTHER_NOTES: (images mein aur interesting details) SIRF filled brief output karo. Koi extra explanation nahi.
